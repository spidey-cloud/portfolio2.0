# Brag Plan

## Angle & Hook
**What is it:** Rohon Kumar Dutta's Portfolio 2.0.
**Visual hook:** A clean, slightly pretentious "we're thrilled to announce" startup vibe from 2016. Light grey backgrounds, friendly blue accents, and an earnest, world-changing tone about a portfolio.
**Tone:** `fake Series A launch from 2016` (Earnest, clean SaaS aesthetic, soft fades, corporate)
**Share caption:** "We are thrilled to announce our Series A and the launch of Portfolio 2.0. By leveraging real-time TOTP authentication and React 19, we are fundamentally rethinking how personal websites scale. Read our Medium post."

## Visual Identity
**Colors:** Light grey background (#f7f9fc), corporate blue (#2b6cb0) for highlights, dark slate text.
**Typography:** Clean sans-serif (Montserrat/Open Sans vibe), regular weights.
**Pacing:** 5 scenes, slow and earnest soft fades.
**Format:** Landscape (1920x1080), 30fps
